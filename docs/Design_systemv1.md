#### 3. Unique Animations & Interactions (None Exist Like This)

**Core Philosophy**: Every animation must **demonstrate capability**.

- **Hero**: Network of glowing nodes (representing connected systems) that light up as you move mouse. Headline text assembles like code.
- **Services**: Cards "build" themselves on scroll (lines draw, icons assemble). Hover → code snippets appear with typing animation.
- **Our Process**: Horizontal/vertical timeline where steps "unlock" with progress ring + real-time "deployment" particle effect.
- **Portfolio**: Projects appear as floating "windows" that expand into full case studies with smooth physics (Framer Motion spring).
- **Capabilities Section**: Interactive "Tech Radar" — hover sectors to reveal depth of expertise with animated stats.
- **Team**: Photos with subtle "pulse" + hover reveals skill bars that fill with smooth counter.
- **Scroll-triggered Global**: Subtle "code rain" or connection lines in background on scroll (very light, performant).

**Tech for Animations**:
- **Framer Motion** (primary)
- GSAP for complex sequences
- Lenis for ultra-smooth scrolling
